🛑 Core Problem
Wind energy is unpredictable, and turbines often:
  • Run at suboptimal rotational speeds
  • Face excessive mechanical stress & wear
  • Waste potential energy due to poor control strategies
💡 Enhanced Solution
An AI-driven control + prediction system that:
  • Predicts wind speed in advance (short-term forecasting)
  • Dynamically adjusts turbine parameters (blade pitch & RPM)
  • Minimizes wear using intelligent RL control strategies

🧠 AI Components (The Core Strength)
1. Wind Prediction
Use: LSTM (Long Short-Term Memory)
Inputs: Wind speed history, Temp, Pressure, Humidity
👉 Output: Future wind speed (next 5–30 min)
2. Optimization Model
Use: Reinforcement Learning (RL)
Goal: Max energy output, min stress on turbine
👉 Actions: Adjust blade pitch angle & rotational speed
3. Wear Prediction 🔥
Use: Regression & Anomaly Detection
Goal: Advance warning for mechanical degradation
👉 Predict: Part failure timing & maintenance schedule
